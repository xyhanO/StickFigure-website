What else does
a camera reveal?

Activity-aware services need motion cues. Raw video carries much more: who is present, what they wear, and what happens in the spaces they call home.

Faces & identity

Faces, expressions, and bystanders can remain visible even when the task only needs body movement.

Clothing & intimate moments

Bedrooms and care settings may capture sleeping, dressing, or receiving assistance—moments people rarely share in public.

Homes & private surroundings

Room layouts, personal belongings, and household context are embedded in the same frames used to recognize an activity.

Privacy leakage beyond the camera.

Every downstream component that receives or retains RGB can also access its appearance-rich content.

See the pose-only interface ↓

StickFigure keeps monitoring RGB local and ephemeral, exporting only a fixed-format pose stream. This reduces appearance-rich exposure; skeleton geometry and motion can still reveal sensitive information. It is not a formal privacy guarantee.

Motion is useful.
Appearance is personal.

For many activity-recognition tasks, articulated body geometry is enough. StickFigure turns camera frames into a compact skeleton stream, keeping raw RGB local and ephemeral during monitoring.
