The idea

Motion is useful.
Appearance is personal.

For many activity-recognition tasks, articulated body geometry is enough. StickFigure turns camera frames into a compact skeleton stream, keeping raw RGB local and ephemeral during monitoring.

Prototype & demo

Built for
the camera.

The measured prototype combines an STM32H753 MCU with an OV2640 image sensor. Local inference produces a compact skeleton stream for downstream activity recognition.

Adaptive execution

Full inference
when motion starts.

A lightweight motion check runs during inactive periods. Detected motion wakes the pose pipeline.
